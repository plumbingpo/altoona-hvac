Restaurants and food-service businesses have climate demands that ordinary equipment can't meet. Kitchens generate enormous heat loads while dining rooms must stay comfortable, exhaust hoods pull out conditioned air that makeup air units must replace, and a busy Friday night pushes rooftop units to their limit. Altoona HVAC specializes in food-service HVAC, including rooftop package units, split systems, proper tonnage and airflow sizing, makeup air balancing against hood exhaust, and scheduled service that works around your hours.

Our service keeps airflow and temperatures where your operation needs them, backed by written reports and service logs after each visit. Those records show a well-maintained facility to landlords and auditors and cover the documentation many commercial leases call for.

Equipment replacements and maintenance schedules are planned with owners, managers, and kitchen leads, so everything fits the hours you're open. If a job can't be done during service, we install during off-hours, keeping your kitchen cooking and the dining room comfortable throughout.

Safe Installation

Safe Installations, Documented Service

Every commercial job we take on starts with safety. Our technicians inspect heat exchangers for cracks, verify proper venting and combustion air, check electrical connections and disconnects, verify the refrigerant charge, clear condensate lines, and measure ventilation rates in occupied areas. The goal is straightforward: protecting the people in your building from CO hazards, poor air quality, and water damage. You receive a written report that ranks each issue by priority and recommends a specific fix.

This work serves a dual purpose: it protects people and property, and it provides the documentation that demonstrates due diligence to landlords, insurers, and auditors. Building owners benefit from maintaining regular professional HVAC service and acting on expert recommendations. We maintain detailed service logs, temperature and filter records, and a documentation binder for every account, with more frequent service for heavy-use equipment in kitchens, gyms, and large mechanical rooms.

Emergency Service

Fast Commercial HVAC Service

Each hour without climate control costs a business money. For a restaurant, it can shut down the kitchen. On a retail floor, it sends shoppers home on a summer afternoon. In a server room, it risks overheated equipment, and in a leased building it invites tenant disputes. Altoona HVAC offers fast emergency HVAC service for commercial properties across Altoona and surrounding areas, dispatching an experienced technician quickly when you call.

Commercial emergencies come in many forms. It might be a failed compressor, a burned-out condenser fan motor, a refrigerant leak, a faulty ignition system or gas valve, a tripped high-limit switch, a frozen coil, or a control board stuck in lockout. Whatever it is, we find it fast, restore heating or cooling, and document the entire call for your facility files. Then we put maintenance and monitoring in place to keep it from happening again.
